This summer, we want to invite other young people into forests, fields, and everyday community life. We want to run, breathe, cook, and get to know the land beneath our feet and the food we eat each day. We also want to talk with one another about a question: can we do something real for nature, our communities, and the way we will live in the future?

— Nate, Founder of R-Gan Junior, and Tianshi Zhang, Co-creator

Over the years, we have come to know Tieniu Village's orchards, fishponds, land, food, and healthy daily routines. We have also seen the close connections among newcomers, longtime villagers, ecological agriculture, and urban families.

Gradually, we came to share a feeling: we have plenty of knowledge and no shortage of information online. What we lack are opportunities to spend time in nature, take part in everyday life, and experience the real world firsthand.

Soon, we will head to college and into a wider world. Looking back at this point in our lives, we realize how much the countryside, nature, and community life here have given us. They have helped us slow down and understand ourselves. They have also taught us about people, land, and food in ways our textbooks never did, and helped us discover our own responsibilities and ways to act.

So this summer, together with our friends, we launched the R-Gan Junior Life Co-creation Camp. We want to invite more classmates and friends from cities to Nanbao Mountain and Tieniu Village to experience a different way of spending time together.

02

Why Are We Starting This Camp?

Because we think many of us are feeling worn out.

Our days are filled with exams and homework. When a break finally comes, all we want to do is stay in our rooms, scroll on our phones, and play games. The more our parents push, the less we want to talk and the more tightly we shut our doors. Sometimes we want things to change, but just cannot find the energy.

We think we need somewhere we can truly catch our breath, without having to compete and keep up every day.

For us, Tieniu Village and Nanbao Mountain are places like that.

That is why we started the R-Gan Junior Life Co-creation Camp this summer. We want to:

  • Run through the forest and let our bodies relax;

  • Light a cooking fire and make good food in the village, experiencing healthy meals without relying on takeout;

  • Talk together about something exciting we could do for the world.

Day 1 | Arriving at Nanbao Mountain: Press Pause

We arrive at Nanbao Mountain in the afternoon, put down our bags, and walk through ancient, moss-covered woodland. This is the beginning of our break from the pace of city life. In the evening, we gather for a welcome in the forest.

Around a campfire and in the mountain breeze, we get to know one another without the awkwardness of formal introductions.

Day 2 | Exploring Nature: Reawaken Your Senses

In the morning, we hike and exercise in the forest. Ferns and mist make the surroundings feel wonderfully open and fresh. We play in nature and let our bodies loosen up as we walk.

In the afternoon, we have a surprise planned: a visit to an organic tea garden in the mountains for a mindful tea-making experience, with time to let go of our worries. In the evening, we explore a valley of fireflies and experience the magic of a summer night.

Part Two | Fields · Soil · Everyday Life

Day 3 | Getting to Know Tieniu Village: Farming and Cooking

We return to Mykunda Community in Tieniu Village. Life is not found only in textbooks and on screens; it is also rooted in the soil. We visit an orchard to learn how citrus grows. We pick vegetables and head into the community kitchen to prepare dinner for one another.

By doing the work ourselves, we want to understand how the food in a meal and a single piece of fruit grow from the land and become the nourishment that sustains us.

Mentor support: Mykunda Community members and ecological agriculture practitioners.

Part Three | Co-creation · Responsibility · Action

Day 4 | Creating Together with R-Gan Junior: Taking Part Firsthand

This is our main day of action. We explore R-Gan Junior's themes in groups based on our interests:

  • Ecology and Nature: Observe an ecological orchard firsthand and investigate the relationship between farming and the land.

  • Technology and AI: Explore how digital tools could help rural communities solve specific problems.

  • Documentation and Visual Storytelling: Photograph and film community members and their stories, practicing how to communicate through images.

  • Society and Community Issues: Investigate real challenges in rural consumption and community life.

  • Expression and Music: Organize research findings and prepare a presentation or musical performance.

Mentor support: Shi Guoping (Tieniu Village's chief planner, guiding community-building and discussion of the bigger picture), ecological agriculture experts, and visual communication mentors providing focused skills guidance.

Day 5 | Sharing and Looking Ahead: Bring What You Have Learned Back to the World 

In the morning, we hold a R-Gan Junior sharing session at Tieniu Village's community reading room. Everyone can share their reflections in their own way:

What have I seen? What matters to me?

What do I want to do next?

These reflections will be the starting point for what we do afterward. In the afternoon, we invite parents to a gathering with music and tea to share the joy of what we have created together. We return to our cities with new insights, like-minded friends, and ideas for a shared future.

What Will Young People Take Home from Camp?

We hope they will take home:

  • Time spent truly relaxing among mountains and the scent of tea;

  • Experience working on a real project, from identifying a problem to developing a solution together;

  • Like-minded friends to keep learning and growing with;

  • A record of personal growth through experiences with the land, their inner lives, and the real world;

  • An opportunity to explore future academic interests, with support for continuing to put ideas into practice;

These records of growth emerge naturally from what young people have actually experienced.

Who We Hope Will Join Us

If you are 12–18 years old,

you are welcome at R-Gan Junior camp!

  • You want to take a break from city life and unwind in forests and fields;

  • You are willing to meet new friends, play together, and take on something meaningful;

  • You are curious about nature, food, technology, photography and film, music, ecology, or social issues;

  • You do not have to be outgoing, just willing to open up at your own pace;

  • You do not need to have everything figured out, just be willing to take part in something real.

Parents Are Welcome, Too

The camp welcomes parents and is designed for families with young people ages 12–18. Parents do not need to watch over every activity. We have our own peers, tasks, and room to grow. Parents will have their own opportunities to unwind in nature, gather for tea, talk, and learn together.

During these days, young people explore and take action with peers. Parents also have time in the mountains and forest, tea gatherings, and conversations of their own. We share the journey while each following our own pace.

We support our child's initiative in starting this camp, and his father and I will also join the team helping to run it. We look forward to welcoming more families and supporting our children as they discover a wider world through real experiences.

One Last Thing We Want to Say

So many young people are busy today.

We have so much homework, so many exams, and pressures we struggle to put into words.

Sometimes we retreat into our rooms and our phones.

Sometimes we long to be understood, yet every conversation seems to take us further away from our parents.

We want to find another possibility.

We do not want lectures or to have everything planned for us.

We want to head into the forest with people our own age, to run and laugh;

To walk into the fields, touch the soil, smell food cooking, and feel connected to everyday life;

And to ask together: what can our generation do for nature, our communities, and the world?

This summer, we invite you to Nanbao Mountain and Tieniu Village.

Among forests, fields, and friends, rediscover strength in your body, light in your heart, and the courage to face the world.

R-Gan Junior is ready to set out with you.
